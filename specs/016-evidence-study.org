- The four techniques the research backs, each named with its source:

1. *Interleaving* — =org-learn-review-interleaved= walks the SAME due
   queue as =org-learn-review= (same SM-2 grading) but interleaved:
   a deterministic day-seeded Fisher-Yates shuffle plus a repair pass
   that spreads same-topic neighbors apart
   (=org-learn-study--interleave=, pure).  Sources: Taylor & Rohrer,
   "The Effect of Interleaving Practice", Applied Cognitive
   Psychology 2010 (interleaving doubled next-day test scores;
   https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/psy_facpub/1760); Rohrer et al.,
   "A randomized controlled trial of interleaved mathematics
   practice", 2020 (https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2019-26066-001).
2. *Feynman technique* — =org-learn-feynman= files a dated child
   heading with a plain-language explanation; subheadings never enter
   the review prompt, so reviews stay prompt-first (active recall).
   Sources: fs.blog/feynman-technique; University of York study
   guide, subjectguides.york.ac.uk (explain as if to a child).
3. *Deliberate practice* — =org-learn-practice= clocks a focused
   session with an explicit LEARN_GOAL; =org-learn-practice-end=
   files the reflection (goal + what improved/next).  Sources:
   Ericsson, Krampe & Tesch-Römer 1993 (Psychological Review);
   Ericsson 2008 (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18778378).
4. *Pomodoro* — =org-learn-pomodoro= runs focus/break cycles over
   org-timer; phase changes notify through the study notify seam.
   Honest caveat (the spec discloses it): the evidence for timed
   blocks themselves is thinner than for the techniques above; the
   cycles exist to make sessions startable and bounded.

* Law

- Seams only: clock, timer and notifier are defcustom functions; the
  ERT suite never needs a live clock, timer or daemon.
- The session reuse is explicit: interleaving reuses
  =org-learn-review--collect-items=, =--entry-content=,
  =--prompt-quality= and =--process-entry= — one scheduler, many
  orders.
