* Mode: =org-learn-ai-guide= (GUIDE)
- Interactive, current subtree, multi-turn in *org-learn-ai-chat*.
- NEVER-ANSWER LAW: the guide probes with open-ended questions,
  decomposes problems step by step, and checks understanding before
  revealing anything.  When the learner asks for the answer outright,
  it climbs a hint ladder: nudge (1st ask) -> scaffold (2nd ask) ->
  worked example (3rd ask, delivered only after the learner has
  written an attempt in that turn).
- PROMPT LAW: the system prompt encodes the five LearnLM learning-
  science principles verbatim-named — active learning, cognitive-
  load management (one idea per turn), adapt to the learner,
  stimulate curiosity, deepen metacognition — plus the never-answer
  law and the hint-ladder contract.
- LOOP: probe -> read-string learner answer -> grade-without-
  revealing + one strength/one-gap line -> next probe (adapted to
  the answer).  =C-g or empty answer exits the loop.

  mapped to org-learn-review--process-entry scheduling; reuse its
